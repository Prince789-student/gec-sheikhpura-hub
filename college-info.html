About Government Engineering College Sheikhpura

A premier state government engineering institution in Bihar, established in 2019 to provide quality technical education and research opportunities.

🏛️ Premier State Government Engineering College Estd. 2019 • DSTTE Govt. of Bihar

Complete Institutional Profile & Campus Guide

Government Engineering College Sheikhpura is an autonomous government technical institute established under the visionary “Saat Nischaya” initiative of Bihar. Below is the complete verified information covering governance, departments, facilities, student cells, and campus infrastructure.

Affiliation & Approval
BEU Patna & AICTE
Affiliated to Bihar Engineering University, approved by AICTE New Delhi.
Academic Branches
6 B.Tech Branches
CSE, Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, ECE, and Mechatronics Engineering.
Approved Intake
360 Seats Annually
Admissions conducted strictly via BCECEB UGEAC based on JEE Main ranks.
Campus Facilities
11 In-Campus Amenities
Central Library, High-Speed Wi-Fi, Hostels, Sports Complex, and Gym.

Campus Infrastructure

Facilities & Student Amenities (11 Official Facilities)" id="facilities

Infrastructure designed to support rigorous academic learning, laboratory experimentation, sports, and resident campus life.

Central Library

Official ↗

Extensive collection of standard engineering textbooks, reference volumes, subscription to IEEE journals, National Digital Library (NDL) connectivity, and quiet reading halls.

Computer Center

Official ↗

Multi-core workstations, gigabit local network, dual-boot Linux/Windows environments, programming IDEs, and computer lab assistants for coding practices.

Campus Wi-Fi Network

Official ↗

High-speed optical fiber connectivity covering academic blocks, faculty rooms, and hostel premises with student authentication.

Hostels (Boys & Girls)

Official ↗

Dedicated on-campus hostel blocks for male and female engineering students with 24/7 security, warden supervision, clean drinking water systems, and cooperative student mess.

Sports & Athletics

Official ↗

Outdoor grounds for Cricket, Football, and Volleyball; indoor court facilities for Table Tennis, Badminton, Chess, and Carrom. Annual sports meets are organized each session.

College Gymnasium

Official ↗

Modern health & fitness center equipped with strength training equipment, free weights, and cardio machines for student physical fitness.

Medical & First Aid

Official ↗

On-campus dispensary for first-aid emergency medical care with immediate referral coordination to Sadar Hospital Sheikhpura (3.8 km away).

Institutional Guest House

Official ↗

Furnished guest rooms for visiting university examiners, guest faculty, recruiters, and official administrative dignitaries.

Account & Bank Section

Official ↗

College accounts desk for fee challans, no-dues verification, PMS scholarship disbursements, and student credit card (MNSSBY) coordination.
