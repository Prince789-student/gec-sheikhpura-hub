B.Tech First Year (4 Years)

The UGEAC Admission Process

Admissions to all 360 seats at GEC Sheikhpura are conducted centrally through the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB).

1

Appear in JEE Main

Candidates must appear in the JEE (Main) Paper-1 examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Passing 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science is mandatory.

2

BCECEB UGEAC Form

Apply online on the BCECEB official portal for Under Graduate Engineering Admission Counselling (UGEAC) using your JEE Main application number and state domicile details.

3

Choice Filling & Locking

Fill your preferred college and branch options online. Add GEC Sheikhpura branches (CSE, CE, EE, ME, ECE, Mechatronics) according to your career interest and lock the choices before the deadline.

4

Provisional Allotment

Download the provisional seat allotment order from the BCECEB portal once the merit list is published for Round 1 or Round 2 counselling.

5

Check Slip & Biometric Verification

Download the official UGEAC Check Slip & Biometric Verification Form and report in-person to the nodal verification center at GEC Sheikhpura campus.

6

Document Verification & Fee Payment

Report to the assigned nodal reporting center with all original documents, biometric verification slip, and pay the state government tuition fee to secure the seat.

LE

Lateral Entry (BCECE-LE)

Diploma engineers can apply for direct 2nd Year (3rd Semester) admissions through the state-level BCECE-LE competitive exam under the 10% supernumerary quota.

Approximate Government Fee Structure

As a Bihar State Government institution, tuition fees are heavily subsidized to ensure affordable technical education:

Annual Tuition Fee
~₹10,000
Per academic year (Subsidized by Dept. of Science & Tech, Bihar)
Hostel & Maintenance
~₹12,000
Per year for in-campus hostel accommodation (subject to seat availability)
BEU Exam Registration
~₹3,500
Per semester exam fee paid directly to Bihar Engineering University

Financial Aid & Bihar Student Credit Card (MNSSBY)

Enrolled students can avail education financing up to ₹4,00,000 (4 Lakhs) under the Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme (BSCCS / MNSSBY) to cover tuition, hostel, mess, and laptop expenses at nominal interest rates (1% for girls/PWD, 4% for boys). Students can apply online through the MNSSBY portal and complete verification at District Registration & Counselling Centre (DRCC), Sheikhpura with bonafide certificates issued by the college.
