Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. Guidelines regarding institutional marks, user-generated contributions, fair use in educational contexts, and formal takedown notices.

1. Institutional Names, Marks & Nominative Fair Use

The names "Government Engineering College Sheikhpura", "GEC Sheikhpura", "Bihar Engineering University", and associated institutional crests are the property of their respective statutory bodies, the Government of Bihar, and university authorities.

Nominative Fair Use Statement: References to GEC Sheikhpura on this website are made solely under the doctrine of nominative fair use for descriptive, geographical, and educational community identification by enrolled students. This website makes no claim of ownership over official college trademarks.

2. User-Generated Content & Student Submissions

Students who publish content on the Hub—such as club banners, sports sparring announcements, or second-hand textbook listings:

  • Retain full original copyright ownership of their uploaded text, photography, and original creative designs.
  • Grant the Hub a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to display, index, format, and broadcast the listing for the duration it remains active.
  • Represent that they own or have obtained permission to use any image or contact detail included in their submission.

3. Academic Partner Resources

All academic study guides, BEU examination question papers, semester solutions, and course outcomes linked on our platform are hosted and curated by Apna College Bihar (apnacollegebihar.online).

Inquiries or copyright questions concerning academic lecture notes and solutions should be addressed directly to the Apna College Bihar academic editorial team.

4. Copyright Infringement Notice & Takedown Procedure

We respect intellectual property rights and promptly investigate any claim of unauthorized content distribution. If you believe your copyrighted work or photograph is appearing on this platform without authorization:

Submit a Written Notice Containing:
  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  2. The exact URL (e.g. gecsheikhpurahub.in/rooms-pg.html#item-id) where the infringing material is located.
  3. Your full name, contact telephone number, and official email address.
  4. A statement that you hold a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner.
  5. A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in your notice is accurate.

Email your formal notice to: contact@gecsheikhpura.in with the subject [Copyright Takedown Notice]. Our volunteer administrator will review and remove verified infringing material within 36 hours.
