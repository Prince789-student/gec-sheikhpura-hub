Academic Requirement

BEU Mandatory Internship Guidelines

Every B.Tech student must complete industrial training / internship following the 4th and 6th semesters to earn academic credits.

Internship Stage Ideal Timing Recommended Sectors & Organizations Deliverables Required
Level-I: Basic Industrial Orientation After 4th Semester (Summer Break - 4 Weeks) Indian Railways (Workshops / Loco Sheds), BSNL Telecom Centers, Local PWD / Electricity substations, or virtual AICTE verified tracks. Training Certificate + 10-page spiral report verified by departmental faculty mentor.
Level-II: Advanced Project Internship After 6th Semester (Summer Break - 6 to 8 Weeks) Core public sector units (IOCL, NTPC, NHAI), tech software firms, IIT/NIT summer research fellowships, or certified startups. Project code / drawings + Presentation before departmental external examiner.
