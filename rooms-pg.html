Student Rooms, PGs & Hostels

Find verified student housing near Bajidpur campus, Police Line, and Bypass Road with realistic rent, security deposit terms, and contact numbers.

CRITICAL ANTI-FRAUD WARNING FOR STUDENTS & PARENTS:
  • Never send advance booking money via UPI/PhonePe to anyone claiming to be a property owner on the internet or WhatsApp.
  • Visit the room in person in Bajidpur or Sheikhpura town. Inspect drinking water, electricity meter rates, bathroom hygiene, and room locks.
  • Always collect a written rent receipt with owner signature and clear terms for deposit refund before shifting in.
