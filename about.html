About the Student Life Hub

An open, student-founded community initiative built by engineering students to solve daily campus living challenges in and around Sheikhpura, Bihar.

Why Was This Hub Created?

When students arrive at Government Engineering College (GEC) Sheikhpura’s campus in Bajidpur, finding verified private room rentals, dependable tiffin delivery services, nearby photocopy shops, and reliable transit options was historically scattered across word-of-mouth WhatsApp groups and unverified posters.

The GEC Sheikhpura Student Life Hub was created to give students a centralized, peer-verified directory for non-academic living. From finding a single room or PG near the college gate to buying second-hand engineering drafters and discovering college clubs, this platform empowers students to navigate campus life smoothly.

Strict Independence & Academic Separation

This hub is strictly a non-academic campus-life project. It does not host syllabus documents, semester question papers, or academic notes.

All engineering syllabus, BEU notes, lecture materials, and PYQs are exclusively managed on Apna College Bihar ↗.

Our Core Principles

🔍 Verified Local Data

We require phone call confirmation or peer inspection before awarding a verified badge to shops, mess providers, or room landlords.

♻️ Student Re-use Economy

Our physical marketplace facilitates peer-to-peer exchanges for cycles, calculators, coolers, and books, saving students money and reducing waste.

🔒 Privacy-First Design

Zero third-party tracking scripts, zero raw IP storage, and strict automated redaction of sensitive credentials in operational logs.

🤝 Community Accountability

Every listing includes a one-click correction button. Students actively curate room availability and shop contacts.

Unofficial Disclaimer

GEC Sheikhpura Student Life Hub is an independent volunteer initiative developed and operated by current engineering students and alumni. It is NOT an official publication, representative, or department of Government Engineering College, Sheikhpura, nor is it officially endorsed by the Department of Science, Technology and Technical Education (Government of Bihar) or Bihar Engineering University (BEU).

For official academic circulars, enrollment, semester fee payment, or university administration, please visit the official institutional website at gecsheikhpura.ac.in.
