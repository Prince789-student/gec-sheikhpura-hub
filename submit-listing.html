Home / Owner Listing Portal

List Your Business or Room

Are you a local shopkeeper, mess provider, or room owner in Sheikhpura or Bajidpur? Submit your listing directly to reach hundreds of GEC Sheikhpura students.

Structured Moderation & Verification Workflow

All submissions are reviewed by student moderators. Submitting a phone number or photo does not guarantee an immediate "Verified" badge. We confirm public contact details and road distance before activating listings. Private identity documents or personal home addresses are strictly kept confidential.

Local Business Registration

Eligible categories: Stationery, photocopy, grocery, laptops & mobile repair, restaurants, tiffin, salons, laundry, plumbers, clinics, and transit.

Direct image link (JPG/PNG). Image files must not exceed 5MB.

Student Room / PG Accommodation Listing

List your available single rooms, double-sharing rooms, student lodges, flats or PGs around Bajidpur campus.

Mandatory Anti-Fraud Policy: GEC Sheikhpura Hub advises students to inspect rooms physically and never transfer advance deposits digitally. We do not expose private personal papers or residential home records.
