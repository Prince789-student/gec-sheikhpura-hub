Privacy Policy & Data Ethics

Last Updated: October 2026. This policy explains how student and visitor information is processed, stored, and protected on this platform under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

Table of Contents

1. Information We Collect 2. Student Accounts & Registration 3. User-Generated Content 4. Technical Information & Logs 5. Cookies & Analytics Disclosure 6. Storage, Supabase & Security 7. Data Sharing & Third Parties 8. Data Retention & Deletion 9. User Rights (DPDP Act 2023) 10. Grievance Redressal & Contact

1. Information We Collect

GEC Sheikhpura Student Life Hub is an educational, non-commercial community platform designed by engineering students for peer utility. We adhere strictly to data minimization principles: we collect only data that is directly necessary to maintain student directory searches, housing listings, campus events, and student welfare features.

We do not sell personal information, build commercial advertising profiles, or monetize student activity.

2. Student Accounts & Registration Information

When you register for a student or provider account, we collect:

  • Display Name: Your chosen name or alias used on public posts and comments.
  • Contact Email: Your email address, used for authentication, account security notices, and password recovery.
  • Academic Profile (Optional): Enrolled department (e.g. CSE, Civil, Mechanical, EE), batch year, and enrollment status.
  • Authentication Credentials: Encrypted cryptographic password hash handled via Supabase Authentication. We never have access to or store your plain-text password.

3. User-Generated Content & Public Postings

When interacting with public directories, you may submit content that is intentionally visible to fellow students:

  • Room & PG Listings: Room title, address in Bajidpur, monthly rent, amenities, and owner contact number.
  • Food & Tiffin Services: Menu details, monthly meal subscription charges, delivery radius, and phone number.
  • Campus Announcements & Events: Activity descriptions, organizer names, venue locations, and dates.
  • Lost & Found Items: Item description, location found/lost, and designated recovery drop point (e.g. campus guard desk).

4. Technical Information & PII Redaction

To prevent unauthorized automated scraping, mitigate denial-of-service abuse, and debug interface errors, minimal operational data is generated during requests:

  • Browser user-agent string and screen dimensions to deliver appropriate responsive layouts.
  • Error stack traces for failed client-side actions.
  • Timestamp and endpoint requested for performance monitoring.
🔒 Automated PII Redaction Guard: All diagnostic logs automatically sanitize sensitive fields. Passwords, JWT auth tokens, authorization headers, and contact phone numbers are converted to [REDACTED] prior to storage or inspection.

5. Zero Commercial Tracking & Cookies

We respect student digital privacy:

  • No Commercial Ad Tracking: We do not run Google AdSense, Meta Pixel, or any third-party behavioral profiling scripts.
  • Functional Local Storage: Preferences such as your dark/light theme, saved bookmarks, and offline drafts are stored strictly inside your own device browser’s HTML5 localStorage. For complete key details, review our Cookie & Storage Policy.

6. Data Storage, Supabase & Security Standards

Data collected is stored using enterprise-grade cloud database infrastructure provided by Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security).

  • Data in Transit: All web traffic is encrypted end-to-end via Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Database tables enforce cryptographic security rules ensuring students can only edit or delete their own listings and submissions.
  • Access Controls: Administrative consoles require multi-factor token credentials; access is strictly restricted to authorized student moderators.

7. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We never sell, rent, or lease student information to any commercial advertiser or external broker. We only share data under the following circumstances:

  • Public Community Display: Information explicitly marked for public listing (room amenities, landlord phone numbers, club meeting dates).
  • Apna College Bihar Integration: Academic syllabi and study notes are hosted independently on Apna College Bihar. Navigating to external partner sites is governed by their respective privacy policies.
  • Legal & Statutory Compliance: In response to lawful orders or statutory requests issued by law enforcement authorities under Indian Law.

8. Data Retention & Deletion Protocols

We keep data only as long as needed for campus utility:

  • Expiring Announcements: Campus life notices and lost-and-found items automatically archive or purge after 30 to 60 days.
  • Self-Service Erasure: You can delete any active room or marketplace listing directly from your account dashboard.
  • Account Removal: For complete profile purge, follow the procedures outlined in our Data Deletion Policy. Formal erasure is completed within 7 business days.

9. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you hold the following statutory rights regarding your personal information:

Right to Access: Request a summary of your personal data processed by the platform.
Right to Correction: Request prompt correction of misleading, inaccurate, or outdated records.
Right to Erasure: Request permanent removal of your account, contact details, and listings.
Right to Grievance Redressal: Have your privacy concerns addressed by our designated officer.
