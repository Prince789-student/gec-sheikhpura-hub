Transport & Connectivity Directory

How to reach GEC Sheikhpura campus at Bajidpur from Patna, Bihar Sharif, Gaya, and Kiul. Verified auto fares, bus terminals, and repair shops.

Sheikhpura Railway Station (SHK)

4.8 km Road Distance from Campus

Situated on the Kiul–Gaya line of East Central Railway (ECR). Regular passenger and express trains connect to Gaya Junction, Kiul Junction, Patna (via Mokama / Bakhtiyarpur), and Bhagalpur.

Fare to Campus:
• Shared E-Rickshaw: ₹20 – ₹25
• Reserve Auto: ₹100 – ₹120
View on Google Maps ↗

Sheikhpura Bus Stand (Chandi Mor)

4.2 km Road Distance from Campus

Major bus and traveller depot on the main bypass. Direct buses and shared travellers depart every 30–45 minutes towards Patna (ISBT Bairiya / Mithapur), Bihar Sharif, Nawada, Barbigha, and Lakhisarai.

Travel Time to Cities:
• Patna: ~3 to 3.5 hrs (₹140 - ₹180)
• Bihar Sharif: ~45 mins (₹40 - ₹50)
• Barbigha: ~25 mins (₹25 - ₹30)
View on Google Maps ↗

Bajidpur Campus Auto & Tuk-Tuk Stand

300 meters from Main Gate

Dedicated e-rickshaw stand outside the Police Line and college approach road. Operating continuously throughout the day between 6:30 AM and 8:30 PM.

Frequent Routes:
• Campus ⇄ Bypass Mor: ₹10 shared
• Campus ⇄ Main Bazaar: ₹15 - ₹20
• Campus ⇄ Sadar Hospital: ₹15
