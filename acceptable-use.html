Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Last Updated: October 2026. Clear rules governing access to our community services, submission forms, and student communication channels.

1. Purpose & Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy establishes the standards of behavior for all students, faculty members, local accommodation providers, and public visitors interacting with the GEC Sheikhpura Student Life Hub.

By accessing any directory, submitting a listing, or interacting with our community boards, you agree to comply with this policy and the provisions of the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India).

2. Permitted Uses

  • Searching verified local accommodations, private hostels, and mess providers in Bajidpur and Sheikhpura.
  • Listing genuine student accommodations, shops, or services with accurate photographs and verified telephone contacts.
  • Organizing friendly campus sports matches (badminton, cricket, volleyball, chess) and registering for student-led society events.
  • Posting genuine campus-life announcements (club meetings, auditions, lost & found items).
  • Submitting factual corrections or flagging outdated phone numbers to help fellow students.

3. Strictly Prohibited Activities

Engaging in any of the following activities will result in immediate content removal, listing cancellation, and permanent account suspension:

Impersonation & False Authority: Pretending to represent college administration, the Principal, faculty professors, proctorial board, or official university bodies.
Fraudulent & Ghost Listings: Posting fictitious rooms, inflated rent prices, bait-and-switch accommodation descriptions, or someone else's personal phone number without consent.
Automated Scraping & DDoS Attacks: Deploying aggressive bots, mass automated scrapers, or load-testing scripts that disrupt service availability for students on mobile networks.
Commercial Spam & MLMs: Promoting multi-level marketing schemes, essay-writing mills, paid assignment rings, or unrelated outside commercial ads.
Malicious Code & Exploitation: Attempting to inject XSS payloads, bypass database Row Level Security (RLS), access admin endpoints without authorization, or intercept private student queues.

4. Responsible Security Disclosure

If you are an engineering student or security researcher who discovers a software vulnerability, security misconfiguration, or unhandled input vector on this platform:

Please report it responsibly to contact@gecsheikhpura.in before public disclosure. We recognize and acknowledge student white-hat contributors who help safeguard community data.

5. Enforcement & Appeals

Our volunteer moderation desk conducts ongoing audits. If your listing or account is flagged:

  • Minor discrepancies will result in a request for clarification via WhatsApp or phone.
  • Confirmed fraudulent or abusive entries are deleted within 24 hours.
  • To appeal an automated suspension or mistaken moderation action, submit a request via our Grievance Redressal Desk.
