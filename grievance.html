2. Scope of Grievances Handled

The Grievance Redressal mechanism investigates and adjudicates reports regarding:

  • Listing Fraud & Rental Scams: Landlords demanding upfront advance booking fees without in-person inspection or advertising false hostel amenities.
  • Safety & Anti-Ragging Violations: Peer harassment, intimidation, derogatory posts, or ragging-related threats via student groups or announcement boards.
  • Unauthorized Personal Data (PII): Publication of phone numbers, private photos, hostel room locations, or identity documents without written consent.
  • Copyright & Intellectual Property: Unauthorized reproduction of copyrighted content, institutional insignia misuse, or non-educational infringements.
  • Account Erasure & Rectification: Failure or delay in honoring data deletion requests submitted under our Data Deletion Policy.
  • Tiffin & Vendor Misconduct: Local food or transport providers delivering unhygienic meals, misleading rates, or refusing contracted services to enrolled students.

3. Redressal Procedure & Resolution Timelines

Step 1: Receipt

Submit grievance via web form or official email with listing URL and supporting screenshots.

Step 2: 24–48h SLA

Formal acknowledgement generated along with unique Grievance Tracking Ticket number.

Step 3: 15-Day SLA

Complete inquiry, moderation decision, corrective takedown, and written closure notification.

* Emergency complaints involving immediate student physical security, ragging, or unauthorized intimate media are expedited and acted upon within 24 hours.

6. Jurisdiction & Legal Framework

This platform operates strictly as an intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000. It does not initiate the transmission, select the receiver of the transmission, or modify the information contained in the transmission.

All disputes arising out of or in connection with the platform or this Grievance Redressal Policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts situated in Sheikhpura, Bihar, India.
