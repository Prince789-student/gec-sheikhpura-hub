Community Guidelines & Student Safety

Our standards for building a supportive, welcoming, safe, and transparent campus environment for all batches, freshers, and seniors at Government Engineering College Sheikhpura.

Table of Contents

1. Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Mandate 2. Peer Conduct & Respect 3. Housing & Marketplace Ethics 4. Campus Clubs & Sports Etiquette 5. Reporting & Moderation Enforcement 6. Official Emergency Contacts
Zero Tolerance Statutory Directive

1. Strict Anti-Ragging Mandate

In accordance with the Supreme Court of India directives, UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, AICTE mandates, and Bihar Engineering University (BEU) statutory policy, ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and constitutes a criminal offense punishable by law.

Any student who commits, abets, or passively tolerates acts of intimidation, verbal abuse, physical harassment, or psychological coercion directed against fresher or junior students will face immediate referral to statutory authorities, suspension, or expulsion.

24x7 Anti-Ragging & Police Helplines:
  • National Anti-Ragging Toll-Free Helpline: 1800-180-5522 (24x7 Confidential)
  • National Anti-Ragging Email: helpline@antiragging.in
  • Bihar State Emergency Response (ERSS): Dial 112
  • Sheikhpura District Police Control: 06341-223201

2. Peer Conduct & Constructive Discussion

Students are expected to maintain collegiate mutual respect across all digital interactions:

  • Respect Across Batches: Communicate cordially with batchmates, juniors, and seniors without hostility, regional slurs, or discriminatory remarks.
  • Truthful Information Sharing: Share honest experiences about hostels, mess food quality, and transport fares without spreading unverified gossip or malicious rumors.
  • Zero Impersonation: Do not post messages claiming to speak officially on behalf of the Principal, Wardens, or Head of Departments.

3. Housing & Marketplace Ethics

To ensure fairness and student welfare:

  • Accurate Product Condition: Disclose all physical defects, missing components, or battery wear when listing second-hand calculators, bicycles, or drafting equipment.
  • No Predatory Markups: Second-hand books and supplies should be priced fairly to support fellow students.
  • Prompt Listing Cleanliness: Mark rooms as occupied and items as sold promptly to save peers from making unnecessary calls.
  • Anti-Scam Compliance: Comply with our mandatory in-person inspection rule: never request or pay money before physical inspection.

4. Campus Clubs & Sports Etiquette

Guidelines for club leads, organizers, and participants:

  • Open & Inclusive Participation: All technical, cultural, and sports clubs listed on the hub must welcome students across all departments and years.
  • Sportsman Spirit: Respect referees, team captains, and venue facilities during inter-branch cricket, volleyball, and badminton matches.
  • Environmental Responsibility: For outdoor meets and college festival activities, volunteer organizers must ensure campus grounds remain litter-free.

5. Reporting & Moderation Enforcement

If you observe a violation of these guidelines:

  • Use the "Report" button directly present on each room card, marketplace item, or announcement.
  • Submit a detailed ticket via the Grievance Redressal Desk with evidence or screenshots.
  • Violations result in progressive penalties: listing removal, account suspension, permanent blacklisting, or formal referral to statutory authorities.
