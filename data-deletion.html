Data Deletion & Account Removal Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. You have an absolute right to control your personal information, request complete profile removal, and unpublish your listings.

1. Self-Service Data Deletion Options

You do not need to wait for moderator approval to remove most data you have created:

  • Marketplace Listings: Open your Student Dashboard > "My Published Listings" and click the red Delete Listing button. The item is immediately removed from the live directory.
  • Saved Bookmarks: In the dashboard or Saved Hub, clicking "Remove Bookmark" clears the reference instantly.
  • Browser Cache & Storage: Signing out clears all session tokens from your device's browser memory immediately.

2. Complete Account & Identity Erasure Request

If you are graduating, transferring, or simply wish to permanently delete your student account and all linked records:

How to Request Deletion:
  1. Send an email from your registered account email to contact@gecsheikhpura.in.
  2. Use the subject line: [Data Deletion Request] - Full Name - Registration No.
  3. Alternatively, fill out the form on our Contact & Helpdesk Page selecting the category "Account Deletion Request".

3. What Gets Erased Upon Account Deletion

Once your identity is verified, our database administrators execute a complete purge of:

  • Your profile record (name, batch year, branch, home district, phone number).
  • Your authentication credentials and hashed passwords from Supabase Auth.
  • All event registrations and RSVP records linked to your user ID.
  • Your published marketplace listings and uploaded item images.
  • Any pending visit requests or landlord inquiries.

4. Limited Data Retention Exceptions

Under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021, intermediaries are required to retain certain administrative logs for a limited period:

  • Anonymized security access logs (timestamp and IP range, redacted of personal phone numbers and passwords) are retained for a maximum of 30 days for anti-abuse and rate-limiting diagnostics.
  • Records of previously resolved reports of severe harassment or fraud may be archived for audit compliance if required by authorized student proctorial committees.

5. Processing Timeline

Commitment to Prompt Action: All valid deletion requests are acknowledged within 48 hours and fully purged from our database within 7 business days. You will receive a final confirmation email once the purge is complete.
