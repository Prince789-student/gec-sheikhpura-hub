Campus First-Aid & Medical Protocol

In case of medical emergencies inside the academic block or hostels:

  1. Notify the Hostel Warden or Security Gate: First aid kits containing antiseptics, bandages, burn cream, and basic fever/ORS sachets are kept at the hostel warden room and academic block reception.
  2. Ambulance Transit: For severe cases requiring hospitalization, call 108 or contact Sadar Hospital Sheikhpura (06341-223233). Road travel time from Bajidpur campus to Sadar Hospital is approx. 8 to 10 minutes.
  3. Inform Roommates & Class Representatives: Never leave an unwell student alone in private accommodation.
