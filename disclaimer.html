Website Disclaimer & Status Statement

Last Updated: October 2026. Important notices regarding our independent student community status, non-affiliation with government authorities, and user-generated content standards.

Table of Contents

1. Unofficial Status 2. Content Accuracy 3. Housing & Marketplace 4. Academic Materials 5. Emergency Numbers 6. Limitation of Liability

1. Independent Unofficial Status

The GEC Sheikhpura Student Life Hub (gecsheikhpurahub.in) is an independent, non-profit community project built and maintained voluntarily by engineering students.

Non-Affiliation Notice: This website is NOT an official web portal of Government Engineering College Sheikhpura, Aryabhatta Knowledge University (AKU), Bihar Engineering University (BEU), or the Department of Science, Technology and Technical Education (DSTTE), Government of Bihar. It is not endorsed, funded, or formally approved by any college administration.

For official institutional documents, academic admissions, employment tenders, and university administrative orders, please refer strictly to the official college portal at gecsheikhpura.ac.in ↗.

2. Content Accuracy & Informational Use

Information on this platform—including faculty visiting hours, room numbering, local business contacts, auto-rickshaw fare ranges, and mess timings—is collected through peer submissions, student field contributions, and publicly accessible notices.

While our student editorial team regularly reviews and moderates listings, we make no warranties, representations, or guarantees, express or implied, regarding the continuous accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of any information published herein.

3. Housing, Room Rentals & Marketplace Listings

The Hub serves solely as an informational bridge connecting students with local property owners, mess operators, and peer seniors selling second-hand textbooks and instruments:

  • No Brokerage or Commission: We do not charge fees or receive financial commissions from private landlords, mess providers, or shops.
  • Physical Inspection Required: Students and parents are strongly advised to physically inspect accommodations, verify drinking water, safety amenities, and power backup, and execute written rental agreements before transferring money.
  • No Financial Transactions: This platform does not process rent payments or hold escrow funds. Never transfer deposit amounts to unknown individuals without verification.

4. Academic Materials & External Educational Hub

To maintain architectural clarity and avoid duplication, academic study materials—including BEU curriculum syllabi, previous year questions (PYQs), subject-wise lecture notes, and CGPA tools—are developed and hosted independently on Apna College Bihar.

Links leading to apnacollegebihar.online are governed by their independent privacy policies, terms of service, and academic content licenses.

5. Emergency Services Notice

The emergency contacts listed on our Emergency Directory (such as Sadar Hospital, District Ambulance, Fire Control, and Police Helpline) are compiled from official district administration directories.

In immediate life-threatening medical emergencies or security situations, dial the national emergency number 112 immediately. The Student Life Hub operates as an information directory only and does not provide direct dispatch services.

6. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable Indian law (including the Information Technology Act, 2000), neither the volunteer maintainers, student contributors, nor associated peer developers of GEC Sheikhpura Student Life Hub shall be liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages arising out of:

  • Your use of or reliance on any information contained on this website.
  • Disputes, tenancy agreements, or financial transactions between students and local landlords or businesses.
  • Any temporary unavailability, technical bugs, or network outages.

If you identify any outdated or incorrect listing on this website, please submit a correction notice via our Feedback & Corrections Desk or email contact@gecsheikhpura.in.
