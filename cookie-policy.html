Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. Complete explanation of how cookies and client-side browser storage (localStorage) are used on GEC Sheikhpura Student Life Hub.

1. Honest Architecture: No Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We believe student platforms should be fast, private, and free of commercial tracking. The GEC Sheikhpura Student Life Hub does NOT use:

  • Third-party advertising cookies (e.g. Google AdSense, Taboola, Outbrain).
  • Social media tracking pixels (e.g. Meta / Facebook Pixel, TikTok pixel, LinkedIn Insights).
  • Commercial behavioral profiling or cross-site fingerprinting scripts.

2. What We Use: Functional Browser Storage (localStorage)

Rather than placing tracking cookies on your computer, our modern web application utilizes standard HTML5 Web Storage (`localStorage`) strictly for essential functional features that make the site usable on slow or spotty campus networks:

Storage Key Purpose Category Lifespan
gec_theme Remembers your Light/Dark visual appearance preference. Essential Preference Persistent
gec_bookmarks Saves your bookmarked hostel rooms and nearby shops for offline recall. Functional Persistent
gec_student_session Maintains active student session for submitting listings or registering for sports activities. Authentication Session / Persistent
gec_admin_session Stores verification state for authorized student council moderators. Security / Auth Session
gec_submissions_queue Locally caches pending submissions if server connectivity is temporarily offline. Operational Queue Until Synced

3. Supabase Authentication Storage

When you sign in with your email or Google OAuth via Supabase, the Supabase client library securely caches an anonymous JSON Web Token (JWT) in local storage to prevent requiring password re-entry on every page load. These tokens expire automatically and are never shared with advertising entities.

4. How to Manage or Clear Your Storage

You maintain complete authority over your browser data:

  • From Browser Settings: You can clear cookies and site data for `localhost` or `gecsheikhpurahub.in` at any time via your browser's History > Clear Browsing Data settings.
  • From the Hub Dashboard: Navigating to Student Dashboard allows you to manage or delete individual saved bookmarks and reset custom budget parameters.
  • Signing Out: Clicking "Sign Out" immediately purges all authentication tokens and session keys from local storage.
Questions or concerns about how data is stored? Email our student web team at contact@gecsheikhpura.in.
