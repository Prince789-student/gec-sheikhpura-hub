1. Purpose & Platform Nature

The GEC Sheikhpura Student Life Hub is an independent, non-commercial peer bulletin board and student directory created by engineering students for the campus community of Government Engineering College Sheikhpura.

The platform operates strictly as an intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000. The hub does not own rental accommodations, employ tiffin caterers, run transport services, or broker financial transactions between users.

2. Eligibility & Permitted Users

Access to this platform is open to:

  • Bonafide students, alumni, faculty, and staff of Government Engineering College Sheikhpura.
  • Local service providers, PG/room owners, and shopkeepers in Bajidpur and Sheikhpura offering legitimate services to students.
  • Prospective students seeking verified admissions and campus information.

3. Account Security & User Responsibilities

When creating an account or posting items:

  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.
  • You agree not to share your student login or post listings under another student’s identity.
  • You agree to immediately notify moderators if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.
Mandatory Safety Directive

4. Mandatory Physical Inspection & Anti-Scam Rules

To safeguard students against rental scams, fraudulent advance requests, and false advertising:

  • Never Send Advance Money Remotely: Never transfer booking tokens, security deposits, or advance rent via UPI/QR codes before visiting the physical property in Bajidpur and meeting the landlord in person.
  • Inspect Amenities Personally: Verify electricity supply, water availability, RO drinking water, Wi-Fi speed, and security measures on-site.
  • Campus Safe Zones for Transactions: Second-hand exchanges (books, cycles, scientific calculators) must take place in public campus areas (e.g. Canteen or Main Gate) in daylight.

5. User-Generated Listings & Accuracy

Any student or local owner submitting a listing warrants that:

  • All stated prices, amenities, photos, and contact telephone numbers are accurate and current.
  • The listing does not infringe third-party intellectual property or privacy rights.
  • When a room is rented or an item is sold, the submitter will promptly mark it as unavailable or delete the listing from the dashboard.

6. Prohibited Activities

Users must not engage in any of the following:

  • Publishing abusive, defamatory, harassing, sexually explicit, or ragging-related material.
  • Automated scraping, crawling, or bulk harvesting of student contact numbers or directory entries.
  • Impersonating college officials, faculty, wardens, or administrative staff.
  • Submitting fraudulent reports or fake reviews to defame competing student services.
  • Distributing malware, phishing URLs, or conducting denial-of-service attempts.

7. Third-Party Platforms & Apna College Bihar

This platform maintains a strict boundary with academic services:

All semester notes, BEU syllabus PDFs, previous year question papers (PYQs), and CGPA tools are hosted independently on Apna College Bihar. GEC Sheikhpura Hub does not duplicate or assume liability for third-party academic servers or external links.

8. Disclaimer of Warranties & Limitation of Liability

The platform, its code, and all content are provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis without warranties of any kind, either express or implied.

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable Indian law, the student developers, administrators, and contributors shall not be liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from rental disputes, loss of money sent in violation of our physical inspection rules, service disruptions, or user misconduct.

9. Dispute Resolution & Governing Jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any controversy, dispute, or claim arising out of the use of this website shall be settled amicably via our Grievance Redressal mechanism. Failing amicable settlement, competent courts located in Sheikhpura, Bihar, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

Formal notices should be directed to our Grievance Officer at grievance@gecsheikhpura.in in accordance with our Grievance Redressal Policy.

10. Amendments & Updates

We may revise these Terms and Conditions periodically to reflect updates in campus policies or statutory requirements. Continued use of the platform following the posting of amended terms constitutes acceptance of those revisions.
