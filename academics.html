Campus Reading Rooms & Local Study Facilities

Physical infrastructure facilities located at the Bajidpur campus and in Sheikhpura town:

🏛️ Central College Library (Bajidpur Campus)

Academic Block, 1st Floor • Open Mon-Sat 09:30 AM – 05:00 PM

Features dedicated book bank scheme for semester students, reading hall with 120+ seats, and reference textbooks. College ID card is mandatory for book issues.

🖨️ Nearby Printing, Xerox & Stationery

Bajidpur More & Sheikhpura Town Market • 0.3 km – 2.5 km

Fast photocopy, spiral binding for lab manuals, assignment printouts, and stationery supplies available right outside campus.
