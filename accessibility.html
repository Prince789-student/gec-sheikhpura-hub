Digital Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: October 2026. Our ongoing commitment to ensuring digital equity, screen-reader compatibility, keyboard navigation, and lightweight performance for all students.

1. Commitment to Inclusive Access

At GEC Sheikhpura Student Life Hub, we believe essential campus information—finding a room, contacting emergency services, discovering student clubs, and locating bus schedules—must be effortlessly accessible to everyone, regardless of physical ability, device constraints, or internet speed.

We aim to adhere to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

2. Technical Accessibility Measures Implemented

🎯 Keyboard-First Navigation

All interactive controls—dropdowns, navigation links, modal close buttons, theme toggles, and form fields—can be navigated sequentially using the Tab key, activated with Enter or Space, and dismissed with Esc. Focus indicators are distinctly visible with high-contrast outlines.

👁️ High Contrast & Theme Support

Our design tokens enforce a strict minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for body copy and 3:1 for large display headings. The user can toggle between clean high-contrast Light mode and eye-comfort Dark mode at any time.

📱 Mobile & Touch Target Optimization

All interactive buttons, footer accordion headers, and navigation targets meet or exceed the recommended 44×44 pixel minimum touch area with adequate touch padding, preventing accidental taps on compact 360px–390px mobile viewports.

🗣️ Semantic HTML & Screen Reader Labels

Pages are structured using landmark HTML5 elements (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>) with appropriate aria-label and aria-expanded states for accordion menus and modal dialogs.

3. Bandwidth & Low-Spec Device Optimization

True accessibility in rural and semi-urban campus environments includes digital accessibility for low-resource hardware:

  • Zero Framework Overhead: The application is engineered with pure Vanilla JavaScript and lightweight CSS, keeping initial page load under 150 KB.
  • Offline Resilience: Emergency phone numbers, saved room bookmarks, and recently browsed listings remain accessible even when cellular connectivity drops.

4. Feedback & Accessibility Assistance

If you encounter any accessibility barrier or have difficulty reading any table, form, or document on this website:

Please email our volunteer accessibility team at contact@gecsheikhpura.in with the subject [Accessibility Feedback]. We will prioritize adjustments within 48 hours.
